# ReelRig AI 
AIBrowser-based AI video studio powered by Three.js 3D and 2D Ink engines for creating cinematic 9:16 Shorts and 16:9 story videos directly in your browser.OverviewReelRig AI is a web-based video creation studio designed for narrative short-form and long-form creators. Instead of relying on unpredictable frame-by-frame diffusion alone, ReelRig AI combines deterministic Three.js 3D scene choreography and stylized 2D Ink / Survival Chronicle presets to keep characters, rooms, lighting, and camera framing consistent from shot to shot.Create dark survival chronicles, interactive story hooks, YouTube Shorts, TikToks, Instagram Reels, or 16:9 YouTube episodes straight from the browser - no heavy desktop editing suite required.Launch Live Studio → https://viral-ai-videos.ai.studioKey FeaturesDual Rendering EnginesThree.js 3D Engine (WebGL): Real-time 3D environments and rigged characters featuring anatomically clamped joint animation, floor-grounded walking locomotion, continuous doorway traversal, moody low-key lighting, and unobstructed raycast-assisted camera framing.2D Ink & Survival Chronicle Presets: High-contrast, graphic-novel and documentary-inspired 2D visual styles built for atmospheric storytelling.Built for CreatorsNative Multi-Format Export: Switch seamlessly between vertical 9:16 (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels) and widescreen 16:9 (YouTube) with aspect-ratio-aware camera framing.Cross-Shot Continuity: Preserves room palettes, props, lighting mood, and character state across consecutive shots in the same location.In-Browser Rendering & Audio: Real-time preview and direct .mp4 / video export with synchronized sound effects, pacing, and transitions.Clean Studio Workflow & UXStudio: Focused dark minimalist workspace for scripting, engine selection, live preview, and rendering.Showcase: Curated gallery of 3D and 2D presets with one-click "Load in Studio" templates.FAQ & Quick Start: Built-in documentation covering engines, export tips, browser performance, and storage.Dual-Layer Notifications: Subtle in-app toast updates paired with optional native browser notifications so you know the moment a render finishes.Quick StartOpen ReelRig AI in a modern Chromium/WebGL-enabled browser (Hardware Acceleration recommended for 3D rendering).Choose your rendering engine (Three.js 3D or 2D Ink / Survival Chronicle) and target format (9:16 or 16:9).Write a story concept or load a ready-made template from the Showcase tab.Preview your shots, fine-tune pacing and audio, and export directly to your device.Architecture & Tech StackLayerTechnologyFrontendReact, TypeScript, Vite3D
